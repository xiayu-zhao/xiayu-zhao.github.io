Tianyu Ren, **Xiayu (Summer) Zhao**, and Dr. Houtan Jebelli were among six **Olympiad Medalists** across engineering fields at the 2025 Olympiad in Engineering Science, at the University of Stavanger in Norway, for *"Autonomous Drone-based System for Precision, Non-contact Surface Finishing in Construction."*
