I'm **Xiayu (Summer) Zhao**, a Civil Engineering PhD student and Master of Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I study robotics and AI for construction in the [RAISe Lab](https://raiselab.cee.illinois.edu/), advised by [Dr. Houtan Jebelli](https://cee.illinois.edu/directory/profile/hjebelli), and collaborate closely with [Dr. Yizhi Liu](https://ecs.syracuse.edu/faculty-staff/yizhi-liu).

Previously, I was a Research Engineer at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), developing large-scale robotic construction technologies and control software, with work in digital fabrication, 3D concrete printing, computational design, and algorithm optimization.

## Research

I combine robotics, computer vision, machine learning, and human–computer interaction to improve construction safety, efficiency, and quality.

- **Perception and inspection:** Real-time roof defect segmentation (RRD-SegNet) and sensor fusion for aerial–ground robot teams.
- **Human–robot collaboration:** Natural-language robot control and LLM-driven navigation with terrain-adaptive planning.
- **Control and autonomy:** Modular UAV–UGV systems, adaptive hexapod control, reinforcement learning for navigation, and UAV payload stability.
- **Worker safety:** Transfer learning for heat-strain prediction and exoskeleton effects on metabolic cost.
- **Digital fabrication:** Robotic construction interfaces, 3D concrete printing, computational design, algorithm optimization, and biomimetic morphology simulation.
